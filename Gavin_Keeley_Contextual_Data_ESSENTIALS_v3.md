## Writing voice (load-bearing for any draft)

- Direct, authentic, grounded in real experience. First person sparingly, only where it carries weight.
- Concede the legitimate parts of the counter-argument before pushing back.
- Name people and institutions; do not hide behind abstractions.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length deliberately. Short sentence, long sentence, aside, fragment. No metronomic rhythm.
- Every piece takes at least two clear positions and carries at least one specific detail only I would know (a number from a real document, a name, a date, a decision on a specific day).
- The conclusion should add, not restate the opening.

## Hard limits

- Documents are always .docx, never .md (this essentials file is the exception, by request).
- Body paragraphs fully justified in letters and articles; headings left-aligned.
- No em dashes anywhere. Use full stops or commas.
- No curly or smart quotes; straight quotes only. No emojis or decorative characters in professional documents.
- No AI tells: no throat-clearing intros ("Let me explain", "Here's the thing", "It's worth noting"), no sweeping closes ("Ultimately", "At its core", "In essence", "In conclusion"), no time-period framing ("In today's fast-paced world"), no fake breakthrough labels ("game-changer", "paradigm shift"), no "not X, it's Y" epanorthosis, no paragraph-starting "Furthermore/Moreover/Additionally", no AI comfort vocabulary (delve, leverage, navigate, tapestry, robust, seamless, foster, embark, harness, unlock, drive, journey, landscape, realm).
- Do not list FAIEA (lapsed).
- Do not attribute a bachelor's degree to me or describe me as a university graduate; I hold no undergraduate degree. Standing rests on professional, director, and vocational credentials.
- Do not surface the RDA resignation reasons in public material; the public framing is workload only (voluntary unpaid role consuming 20 to 40 hours a week).
- Lead with QUT for academic positioning; treat the UniSC Adjunct Associate Professor role as past.
- Do not flatter or over-affirm. Substance over politeness.

## Anti-slop discipline

LinkedIn now ranks with a single semantic foundation model (360Brew, feed rollout March 2026) and actively suppresses generic AI content; it named the "it's not X, it's Y" format specifically as a demotion target, so avoid that structure entirely. Widely reported (not officially confirmed) that the model rewards dwell time, saves, and comment depth; treat as directional and optimise for genuine reader value. External links go in the first comment, not the post body (heavy in-body reach penalty). Engagement pods and bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?") now suppress rather than lift.

Three-touch workflow: write my actual take in one messy sentence first; use the model to develop angles, counters, and evidence gaps; then hand-edit the first and last line, always. Pre-publication: would the first line stop the scroll; is there a detail only I would know; are there at least two real opinions; does every paragraph survive the delete test; does it read aloud like something I would actually say.
