Lead On Purpose

Intentional Leadership in a World of Chaos

Here's the truth I've figured out: most leaders have no idea what they're doing. They were never trained. They got promoted because they were good at something else, handed a team, and left to figure it out.

Lead On Purpose doesn't just challenge conventional leadership wisdom. It exposes why it was always destined to fail. Friedman's doctrine is dead. Classical management theory was built for a world that no longer exists.

"You are currently experiencing the fastest rate of change in your life, but it will be the slowest you experience for the rest of your lifetime."

How prepared do you feel to lead through that? This book offers a different path: building an invincible culture that replaces dogma with evidence and pretence with competence.

Evidence first. Values always. Dogma never.

About Me

I've spent four decades in technology and business leadership across four continents. I've been a CTO, a CEO, an executive at companies like Suncorp and Aviva PLC. I've founded companies, sat on boards, advised governments. I've worked in North America, Europe, Asia, and now Australia.

None of that guarantees I know what I'm talking about. But it does mean I've seen the same leadership failures repeat themselves in predictable patterns, regardless of industry or geography.

My scientific background taught me to follow evidence over opinion and hold strong views loosely. That approach has served me better than any management theory I've encountered.

I'm Partner at Circle Leadership Global and Tractor Ventures, Adjunct Professor at Queensland University of Technology, and I've lived on the Sunshine Coast with my family for 20 years. I'm inducted into both the Queensland Pearcey Entrepreneur Hall of Fame and the Australian Computer Society Hall of Fame. I co-founded Group X, which helped drive a 60% increase in ICT enrollment at Queensland universities and became the federal Digital Careers program. I led a seven-year campaign that secured Queensland's undersea fibre optic connection.

I wrote Lead On Purpose because I got tired of watching capable people fail at leadership simply because no one taught them how to do it properly. The book challenges most of what passes for leadership wisdom because most of it is comfortable fiction that doesn't survive contact with reality.

Evidence first. Values always. Dogma never.

For Business Builders Who Know Change Never Stops

Join the Business Builders Club

Whether you're a solo founder, running a team of 20, or the third generation in a family business, the challenge isn't scaling. It's evolution. The world keeps changing faster than most businesses can adapt, and the guru industrial complex keeps selling you growth frameworks that only make them richer.

The Business Builders Club at Circle Leadership brings together first-time founders and 40-year veterans, family businesses and bootstrapped startups. What you share matters: you're all building micro to small businesses (1-20 people) that need to constantly evolve in a VUCA world—volatile, uncertain, complex, ambiguous—without losing what makes you viable.

This is a community, not a cult. We're practitioners who share what actually works when the environment shifts, call out what doesn't, and build the foundations that let you adapt rather than collapse. If you want to scale later, these foundations make it possible. But we're not here to sell you the get-bigger Kool-Aid.

The second Tuesday of each month throughout 2026, I run a 90-minute onboarding session for new members. We work on building resilient businesses that evolve with reality rather than chase the next guru's formula. You get access to a community that's already navigating the same changes you're facing.
